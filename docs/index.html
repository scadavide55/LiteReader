Why LiteReader

Small, quick and private.

Made for tablets that feel slow everywhere else. Pages turn smoothly and the app keeps its memory use low.

Smooth pages

Pinch zoom, double tap and fast page turns, drawn by a native decoder.

Library

Recent books with progress and cover thumbnails, plus your Books and Documents folders.

Bookmarks and resume

Picks up where you left off, with bookmarks and a page grid for jumping around.

Manga or Normal

Right to left or left to right, detected from the file and switchable per book.

Private

No network access, no accounts, no ads. Your files stay on the tablet.

Open source

Source on GitHub under AGPL-3.0, built on MuPDF and libmobi.

Supported files

Opens what you already have.

Other formats show a clear message instead of failing.

CBZcomics EPUBimage comics EPUBtext PDFtext and images, up to 2 GB MOBIno DRM AZW3no DRM DOCXup to 15 MB TXTup to 10 MB

Not supported: DRM-protected books, KFX, DjVu, CHM, and old .doc, ODT or RTF.

Reading

Tap the Menu tab at the top of the page to open the reading menu. From there you can jump with the page grid, add bookmarks, or switch between Manga and Normal.

Your position is saved when you close the app.

Install

Three steps.

Works on Android 4.0 (Ice Cream Sandwich) and newer, on ARMv7 devices.

Allow installs from this source

On the tablet, open Settings → Security and enable Unknown sources.

Download the latest release

Download the APK from the latest release and open it on the tablet.

Open LiteReader

Tap Install, then open the app from the launcher.
